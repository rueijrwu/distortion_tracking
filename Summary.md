# Distortion model summary

The project retains related but distinct optical studies. P1 models rotation-dependent changes from its actual real-coordinate grid at zero rotation, preserving the sampled baseline barrel distortion. Original P4 analyses fit radial distortion across accommodation and model rotation at 0 D. A separate P4 Z study measures image magnification over Z at five accommodations and five rotations. P1 and P4 parameters are setup-specific, and the two P4 datasets were collected from different LEN revisions.

**P1 dataset status:** The canonical P1 grid spans 51 absolute `Cornea_ENT_D` THI values from -5 to +5 mm; the current sequence baseline is 1 mm. The P1 rotation report retains its earlier single-slice provenance and sequence hash, but a numerical audit found its full 3,609-row slice (401 rotations by nine fields) matches the current Z=0 slice to floating-point roundoff. Its reported fit is therefore applicable at Z=0. It does not establish rotation behavior at the other Z planes. The absolute THI coordinates are reported as collected, without conversion to displacement from the 1 mm baseline.

Both rotation analyses use `X=sx*x0/(1+q*y0)` and `Y=sy*y0/(1+q*y0)` over 401 rotations and nine field points. For P1, the zero-rotation fit estimates `(sx,sy,q)=(1,1,-8.23e-18 mm^-1)`. Direct coordinate RMSE ranges from 0 to 5.95020 um (mean 3.06366 um); the in-sample quadratic parameter approximation ranges from 0.0521408 to 5.96587 um. At -20/+20 degrees, direct parameters are approximately `(1.00738400,1.02576045,-0.00738237)` and `(1.00738400,1.02576045,+0.00738237)`. See [Theory.md](Theory.md#p1-fixed-baseline-barrel-plus-vertical-keystone), the [P1 report](data/p1_rotation_transform/report.md), [runner](Script/analyze_p1_rotation_transform.py), and its [coefficient](data/p1_rotation_transform/keystone_coefficients.png) and [spatial-error plots](data/p1_rotation_transform/coordinate_rmse.png).

At zero rotation, the P1 real grid changes across Z almost entirely by isotropic magnification relative to its actual Z=0 grid. The working normalized linear model is `m(Z)=1+0.00295165270232 Z` (Z in absolute THI mm; slope in mm^-1), with ratios 0.9854583393 at -5 mm, 1 at 0 mm, and 1.014977286 at +5 mm. Its ratio RMSE is 1.01275e-4 and maximum absolute ratio error is 2.19022e-4 over the 51 saved Z planes; at theta=0 its largest coordinate RMSE is 0.29549 um and maximum point error is 0.50326 um. For comparison, the identity-near quadratic empirical fit `m(Z)=0.999999995615 + 0.00295165270232 Z + 8.71229730269e-6 Z^2` has ratio RMSE 5.14398e-7 and maximum error 1.21943e-6. A direct check at theta=-10,-5,0,+5,+10 degrees over all 51 Z planes found a maximum factor difference of 1.94155e-6, equivalent to maximum relative variation `|m(theta,Z)/m(0,Z)-1|` of 0.00019129%; using the exact theta=0 scale curve left at most 0.007220 um coordinate RMSE and 0.017804 um maximum point error. This supports angle independence only at those five tested angles, not all 401. The direct per-plane scale fit at theta=0 has maximum coordinate RMSE 0.00120667 um and maximum point error 0.00206133 um; directly scale-normalized grids differ by at most 0.00118886 um coordinate RMSE. The independent radial fit gives `k1(Z)=-0.0130398764628 + 7.68681341348e-5 Z - 1.13154451225e-7 Z^2` in mm^-2, with Z-polynomial coefficient units mm^-2, mm^-3, mm^-4. Its dimensionless normalized `k1*r_edge^2` varies by 0.00898% of its absolute mean, while per-grid radial-fit coordinate RMSE is 2.24–2.31 um. See [Theory.md](Theory.md#p1-fixed-baseline-barrel-plus-vertical-keystone), the [expanded P1 radial and magnification report](data/p1_radial_distortion_fit/p1_radial_distortion_fit.md), [magnification plot](data/p1_radial_distortion_fit/p1_z_magnification.png), and [analysis script](Script/fit_p1_radial_distortion.py).

For P4, the radial model is `real = paraxial * (1 + k1*r^2)`, fitted independently to nine-point grids at each of 51 accommodation values from 0 to 5 D. At 0 D, `k1=-0.01852833121 mm^-2` and coordinate RMSE is `0.00110191 mm`. Across all groups, coordinate RMSE ranges from `0.000534472` to `0.00110191 mm` (mean `0.000807812 mm`). The exponential and quadratic accommodation curves are in-sample summaries of the 51 coefficients; the sampled grid has only two distinct nonzero radii. Full definitions and limits are in [the P4 radial report](data/p4_radial_distortion_fit/p4_radial_distortion_fit.md).

The P4 rotation transform at 0 D has direct coordinate RMSE from 0 to `0.00521325 mm` (mean `0.00120933 mm`); its in-sample quadratic parameter approximation ranges from `0.000286062` to `0.00657821 mm`. Neither transform fit is held-out validation or a measurement of hardware precision. The original P4 radial and rotation analyses are separate; accommodation dependence of the keystone parameters has not been fit. See [the P4 rotation report](data/p4_rotation_transform/report.md) and [Theory.md](Theory.md#p4-optical-model-analyses).

The separate P4 Z magnification study uses `Lens/p4_ME.len`, zoom z1, and `Cornea_ENT_D` with absolute same-sign `THI=Z`. Its loaded z1 baseline readback is 0 mm. It sampled 0, 1, 2, 3, and 4 D; RC rotations -10, -5, 0, +5, and +10 degrees; 51 absolute THI values from -5 to +5 mm in 0.2 mm steps; and nine fields (1,275 states, 11,475 records). For every accommodation and rotation, the magnification reference is that same state's actual real-coordinate grid at Z=0. The measured scale is the pooled dot-product factor `m=dot(R0,RZ)/dot(R0,R0)`; a linear `m(A,Z)=1+alpha(A)Z` is fit to theta=0 ratios and then checked against the other four angles.

Slope variation across accommodation is 0.0818504% of the mean. A common `alpha≈0.00295 mm^-1` is convenient over the sampled range; use the per-accommodation slopes when accuracy matters. The worst linear-model state has 0.249 um coordinate RMSE and 0.511 um maximum Euclidean point error. Pooled linear coordinate RMSE is 0.100–0.106 um across accommodation. Using the directly measured same-angle scalar at each Z instead gives pooled coordinate RMSE 0.0120–0.0160 um and maximum absolute coordinate residual 0.0653–0.0854 um; these residuals are distinct from the constrained linear-model errors. The measured theta=0 endpoint ratios are 0.9854550569/1.014980754 at 0 D and 0.9854667801/1.014968316 at 4 D for Z=-5/+5 mm. These results describe simulated-grid consistency, not hardware precision.
